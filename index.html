Bio-Medical & Public Waste Segregation Protocol

Standard WHO & CPCB color-coded disposal rules to prevent needle-stick injuries and groundwater contamination:

Yellow Bin — Infectious & Anatomical Waste Soiled bandages, cotton swabs with blood/body fluids, expired medicines, discarded linen. (Incineration/Plasma Pyrolysis)
Red Bin — Contaminated Recyclable Plastics IV tubes, catheters, urine bags, syringes (without needles), gloves. (Autoclaving & shredding)
White (Translucent) Puncture-Proof — Sharps Hypodermic needles, scalpels, blades, lancets. Never recap needles manually!
Blue Box — Glassware & Metallic Implants Broken medicine vials, ampoules, glass slides, metallic implants.
